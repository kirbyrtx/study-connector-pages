Privacy Policy

Study Workspace Connector is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Sheets, Docs, Drive files and Apps Script projects on the owner's computer to manage a private study question bank.

  • No data is collected from, or shared with, any other person or third party.
  • Google account data is used only on the owner's computer and is never sold, transferred or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
